Bitbucket DR – Assessment of Backup and Data Synchronization Approach




Hi Team,

Following our consultation with the Bitbucket vendor regarding the Production-to-DR architecture, we have received clarification on the recommended approach.

The vendor recommends dedicated filesystem replication technology between independent Production and DR storage. However, as our current infrastructure provides NFS without SAN-based replication capabilities, we would like to assess the feasibility of an alternative backup-and-restore approach.

The vendor has confirmed that taking a Production backup and restoring it to the independent DR environment is a viable alternative.

We would therefore appreciate your assessment of the following:

- Provisioning and maintaining independent NFS storage for Production and DR.
- Establishing a scheduled backup and transfer mechanism for the Bitbucket shared-home data from Production to DR.
- Assessing whether incremental backups or synchronization can be implemented after the initial full backup to reduce transfer duration and infrastructure overhead.
- Ensuring backup consistency and integrity, including coordination with the Bitbucket database recovery point.
- Defining the achievable backup frequency, retention, monitoring and restoration procedure.

The existing Bitbucket shared-home data exceeds 400GB, so we would also appreciate an assessment of the initial transfer requirements and the feasibility of maintaining subsequent recovery points.

The objective is to establish a practical DR recovery mechanism without introducing a shared filesystem between the Production and DR sites.

Please advise on the available infrastructure capabilities and recommended implementation approach.

Thank you.
