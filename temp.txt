### UCD OIDC – Vendor Validation

**Current status:**
UCD `8.2.1.0.ifix01.1187776` reaches Entra ID successfully in the browser, including MFA, and receives the authorization callback. Failure occurs during the **server-side token exchange**, with:

```text
Requesting auth token: tokenEndpoint=...
failed to parse ID token response
java.net.ConnectException: Connection timed out
```

### Tests completed

**1. Direct connectivity from UCD server**
