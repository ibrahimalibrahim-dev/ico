**Title: UAT Bitbucket – NFS Mount Point Reconfiguration**

**Description:**

As part of the Bitbucket Data Center UAT shared-home configuration, Atlassian has recommended mounting the NFS share directly under the Bitbucket home directory, with the following target structure:

- `BITBUCKET_HOME`: `/bitbucket`
- NFS shared-home mount: `/bitbucket/shared`

Currently, the NFS export is mounted directly at `/bitbucket`, with the existing shared-home contents residing at the export root.

Kindly support with the following:

- Stop Bitbucket prior to the mount-point change.
- Reconfigure the existing NFS mount from `/bitbucket` to `/bitbucket/shared`.
- Retain the existing NFS data without performing another full synchronization or data copy.
- Ensure the required ownership, permissions, and persistent mount configuration.
- Verify that the NFS share is correctly mounted and accessible at the new location.

**Completion Criteria:**

The existing shared-home data is accessible under `/bitbucket/shared`, the NFS mount persists across server restarts, and the previous mount-point configuration is no longer in use. This will allow DevSecOps to update the Bitbucket home configuration and proceed with UAT application validation.
