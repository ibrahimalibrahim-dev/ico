
Bitbucket HA – NFS Shared Home Data Synchronization and Validation




As part of the Bitbucket HA implementation, the shared-home data must be migrated from the existing local filesystem to the provisioned NFS mount. This is a prerequisite for ensuring that the shared data is available through the intended storage location before proceeding with the Bitbucket configuration and subsequent HA activities.

The current transfer attempts have encountered prolonged execution times and Operation not supported errors, requiring OS/Linux Administration support to assess the NFS compatibility and execute the synchronization using an appropriate and reliable method.

Scope of work

OS/Linux Administration is requested to:
1. Review the existing NFS mount configuration and investigate the reported transfer errors.
2. Determine and execute the appropriate synchronization method to transfer the contents of the existing Bitbucket shared directory to the NFS destination.
3. Ensure the destination reflects the source directory, including the required files, subdirectories, ownership and permissions, to the extent supported by the NFS export.
4. Perform the final synchronization after coordinating the required Bitbucket service downtime, ensuring that no application changes are missed during the migration.
5. Validate the synchronized data and confirm that the NFS destination is ready for Bitbucket configuration and testing.
Completion criteria

This subtask can be considered complete when:
- The NFS destination contains a complete and consistent copy of the source shared directory.
- Destination-only files that are not present in the source have been reconciled, where appropriate.
- Required ownership, permissions and supported metadata have been preserved.
- Synchronization and validation have completed without outstanding transfer errors or unexplained discrepancies.
- OS confirms successful completion and provides the final destination mount/path for DevSecOps validation.
Dependency / coordination

DevSecOps will coordinate the required Bitbucket service downtime and perform application-level validation following completion of the OS activity.
