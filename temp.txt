Bitbucket HA/DR – Assess Supported Production-to-DR Data Replication Options


As part of the Bitbucket HA/DR implementation, the vendor clarified that the shared NFS home directory should only be shared between nodes within the same cluster. Production and DR must maintain independent storage.

Following consultation with the Storage team, it was confirmed that storage-level replication is not currently available within our infrastructure; NFS is the available shared-storage solution.

Further assessment is therefore required, in coordination with the vendor, OS and Storage teams, to establish a supported approach for synchronizing Bitbucket repository data between Production and DR, including any backup, replication, consistency and recovery requirements.



Objective: Confirm the feasible implementation options and associated requirements to establish the Production-to-DR data recovery approach.

Dependency: Vendor guidance and OS/Storage team assessment.
