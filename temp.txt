As part of our ongoing work to establish a Disaster Recovery (DR) setup for Bitbucket, we are assessing how the application's data can be maintained and recovered at the DR site in the event of a Production outage.

Following discussions with the Bitbucket vendor, the recommended approach is to use dedicated filesystem replication technology between the Production and DR storage environments. However, given that our current infrastructure provides NFS storage without SAN-based replication capabilities, the vendor has also confirmed that a backup-and-restore approach is a viable alternative.

We would therefore like to assess the feasibility of implementing this approach using our existing infrastructure.
