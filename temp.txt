Assessment and Remediation Planning – Newly Identified Server Vulnerabilities



A new set of server vulnerabilities has been highlighted by IT Cyber Security Management following the completion of the previous UCD Agent and JDK patching preparation activities.

This subtask is being raised separately to track the assessment of these newly identified findings and distinguish them from the vulnerabilities covered under the previous remediation plan.

Activities include:

Review the newly reported vulnerabilities and identify the affected servers and components.

Assess the required remediation measures and any dependencies or potential impact.

Engage the relevant technical owners and vendors where further clarification or support is required.

Establish a revised remediation approach, implementation sequence, and target timeline.

Prepare the necessary CAB submissions and coordinate implementation activities accordingly.

Completion Criteria:

The new vulnerability findings have been assessed, remediation actions and dependencies identified, and an updated implementation plan with target timelines established for the affected servers.
